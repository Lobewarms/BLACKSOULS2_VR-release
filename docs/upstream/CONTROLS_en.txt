BLACK SOULS II VR - Controls  (version 0.2.53)
================================================

New in 0.2.53
- PC sword clicks chain different cuts; a charged blow thrusts. In VR, adjust
  Weapon weight in combat settings to change blade follow-through.
- Running in a fight, dodging and attacks spend stamina. When exhausted, walk
  until enough stamina returns. Lindamea's immortality duel has unlimited stamina.
- In multiplayer, / opens text chat on PC; Enter sends and Esc closes it.
  In VR, use the chat slot in the emote ring or Co-op > Chat and voice.
  Enable your microphone there to talk. You can choose a device or mute players.
- Lift platforms, original fonts/window colors and scrolling text are supported.


Confusion ending correctly
- Curing or ending Rampage stops queued automatic hits, follow-up skills and
  unlaunched gun rounds, and restores manual attacks and skills immediately.
- Already-fired projectiles and paid weapon-skill cooldowns continue normally.

Combat and event fidelity fixes
- Attacks, skills and projectiles now use the original critical scaling, critical
  evasion, counter, reflection and substitution rules. Each repeated strike gets
  its own chance to hit; weapon arts keep their own authored repeat count.
- Bonus Actions remain available when an ordinary action is already ready.
- Enemy skills respect Silence, seals and resources. An enemy with no usable
  action waits. Rampage forces normal attacks rather than permitting manual arts.
- Delayed strikes, NPC cooldowns, map events, movement routes and timers pause
  with local menus. Companion status durations refresh and survive save/load.
- Nested confirmations preserve the original choice group. Original event
  movies play on PC and in VR; use Play/A/Enter if prompted, or Skip/B/Esc.

Grand Guignol Skills menu
- R opens Skills during Grand Guignol and other battles whose entrances disable
  the exploration menu. Dialogue and cutscenes still control when menus can open.

Bonus Action skill chains
- R Skills shows earned Bonus Actions and your queued skills.
- With extra actions available, select skills, then press R again or click Cast
  to use them in order. In VR, use the Cast button. Remove last and Clear edit
  the plan; Esc/Back cancels it without spending actions or resources.
- Each cast spends one earned action and uses its own costs and cooldowns.
  An unavailable skill stops the remaining chain without spending for that cast.
  Temporary weapon switching preserves earned actions and uses the art's stats.

Lindamea battle
- Cinematic backsteps no longer slow your movement during real-time combat.
- Her shared immortality phase shows its remaining time below the enemy HP bar.
  The countdown pauses with the fight when a blocking menu is open.

Performance update
- More responsive Skills browsing with large skill lists and many owned weapon arts.
  Costs, cooldowns and skill requirements stay up to date while you browse.
- Maps with many exits or scenery events do less repeated work during exploration.

USING SKILLS FROM THE MENU
-------------------------
On PC, press R to open Skills directly; press R again to close it. E remains
Examine/Talk. Click a skill, or select it and press Enter, to use it immediately.
Face a nearby visible enemy for attacks; if no valid target is in view, nothing
is spent. In VR, point and confirm with the trigger/A, or use the stick and A.
Quick slots still prepare spells and weapon arts for manual aiming/striking. Healing and
support take effect normally. Dodge and manual Break retain their dedicated
actions. Common-event skills return to the game to run their event.
Costs, weapon requirements, seals and cooldowns still apply. Field-only utility
skills cannot be used during combat. Quick-slot assignments stay as you set them.
Skill cooldowns and shared action recovery pause with the world, including while
R Skills is open. Accepted action-consuming skills, including healing and buffs,
return to gameplay automatically so recovery can progress. Reopening R freezes
the remaining time too. Pure out-of-combat utility can stay open.

FINDING AND FAVORITING SKILLS
----------------------------
Use Favorites, All, Weapon Arts, Attack, Healing, Buffs or Debuffs to narrow the
list. A skill may appear in more than one category. Favorites appear first.
Select a skill and press F, or use Favorite, to mark/unmark it. Q or Quick-slot
opens slot assignment for your usual learned or currently granted skills.
Unavailable rows remain inspectable and favoritable, with current costs and
the reason they cannot be used. Borrowed-only arts use R as described below.
On PC, press / or click the search field, then type a name. Enter or Esc finishes
typing and returns to the list; Clear resets the search. Your favorites, category, search
and selected skill are remembered separately for each character. In VR, use the
visible category, Favorite and Quick-slot controls with the pointer or stick.
Smaller windows show the full current category with previous/next arrows;
Left/Right also changes category. Two lines of the original skill description
are shown, with the complete longer text available on the profile pages.

ARTS FROM WEAPONS IN YOUR BAG
----------------------------
R also lists arts from weapons, offhand guns and shields in your bag. The selected action
shows its source weapon's original icon/name and the costs with that weapon.
Using the art from R chooses a compatible owned weapon for that action, then
your usual equipment resumes. Repeats and projectiles keep that action's weapon
stats and effects. Equip permissions, seals, costs and cooldowns still apply.
Break skills still prepare the next real hit; strike within three seconds.
Disarming cancels that preparation. Compatible equipped gear is preferred;
swapping within a weapon family uses the highest owned +N refinement.
Main profile stats describe your current equipment. Action weapon and skill
costs show what the selected skill will use. Source weapon names are searchable.
Q works for learned or currently granted skills. An art borrowed only from an
unequipped weapon is used directly from R; equip its source weapon before
assigning it to a quick slot. Quick slots keep their usual equipped-weapon rules.

YOUR PROFILE IN SKILLS
----------------------
The Skills menu shows your current party leader's original portrait, HP, MP, TP,
stamina and all eight current stats, including Luck. Page through every active
status effect and buff with original icons, plus useful damage, recovery and cost
modifiers. Healing restrictions and the selected skill's HP/MP/TP costs, cooldown
or reason it cannot be used are shown too.
Use Previous/Next on the profile. On PC, Page Up/Page Down or the mouse wheel over
the profile also turn pages. In VR, point at the page buttons and confirm.
The profile stays beside the skill browser and quick-slot assignment picker.
Smaller PC windows stack the panels and page resources and stats as well.

BUYING LARGER QUANTITIES
-----------------------
Use +/-100 or +/-1,000 when available, or choose Max. Max selects the affordable
amount; confirm on the first row to buy. Your bag holds up to 9,999 per item.
Storage uses its own limits, and exchanges still require their materials.

BONUS ACTION
------------
During combat, after a normal eligible action, each Bonus Action trait rolls once:
+50% is a 50% chance for one follow-up, and +100% guarantees one. Sources stack.
Spend the earned follow-ups on your next attacks or eligible combat skills.
Each follow-up consumes one count and cannot roll more follow-ups. There is no
three-second timeout; unused follow-ups clear when combat ends. The HUD shows
the original source icon and the number of follow-ups left.
Skills share action recovery with attacks. An earned follow-up can begin during
the previous action's trailing recovery. Every action keeps its own full
recovery, so a new normal chain waits until the final follow-up has recovered.
Active attack motions, burst spacing, MP and stamina costs, and skill cooldowns
still apply. Items and utility actions do not count.
This works with PC, VR and skills used directly from the R menu.

BONFIRE TRAVEL
--------------
Highlight a destination to see its position on the original world map and both
description lines. Confirm to travel, or cancel to return. The map and list
rearrange to fit smaller PC windows and keep the same selection when entering VR.
