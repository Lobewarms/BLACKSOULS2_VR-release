BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.7

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Loose books in Library Dream lie flat on desks and the floor.
- Teleport circles show destination names from your game's translation.
- Dropped souls/mana stand upright after death so they are easier to find.
- Corpses and floor skeleton remains have visible thickness.
- Fixed Black Soul and Tainted Black Soul use, including multiple at once.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
