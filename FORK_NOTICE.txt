BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.11

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Navigate with a live minimap and a full area map: press N or open Menu > Map.
- Maps use the game's tile artwork and show your facing direction and named exits.
- Main menu fits one screen, with Character, Save / Load and Settings groups.
- PC menus and HUD stay readable on screen; VR keeps its wrist HUD and spatial menus.
- The launcher has a borderless game-style window, PC / VR choices and new storybook artwork.
- HUD resources have clear labels, matching bars and grouped status icons.
- Click menus and Back on PC, or use the controller pointer in VR.
- Menus use the game's window skin; status effects use the game's own icons.
- Fixed update checks failing after the first successful check.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
