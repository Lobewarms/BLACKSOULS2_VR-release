BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.8

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Get community updates directly from the launcher, with download progress.
- Update downloads are checked before setup opens; saves are kept.
- Includes the original author's latest bonfire travel and launcher folder fixes.
- Keeps the community fixes for books, furniture, remains, weapons and soul items.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
