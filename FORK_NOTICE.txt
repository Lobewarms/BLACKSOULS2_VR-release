BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.12

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- The original menu layout returns: Items, Skills, Equipment, Status, Covenants,
  Save and Game End on the left; character information on the right.
- Mod tools has its own button. Press F1 while the menu is open for Map, Load,
  Co-op and Settings; press N during play for the full area map.
- The compact PC HUD uses original battle portrait artwork, gauges and status icons.
- HUD panels are translucent, and the PC minimap is circular and semi-transparent.
- Resource gauges stay fixed as effects change; priority effects remain visible.
- Item and soul pickups appear quietly at the lower right and repeated items combine.
- Dialogue choices fit their contents and stay above the bottom dialogue window.
- PC weapons rest lower at the side and hide during menus and dialogue.
- Save labels work for PC and VR, with shared saves retained during upgrades.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
