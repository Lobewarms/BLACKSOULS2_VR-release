BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.5

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Desktop swords now wind up, slash across the view and return to a raised stance.
- Fixed missing pistol artwork when a smaller icon sheet hides the game's full one.
  The app recovers missing icons from your own game without changing its files.
- Desktop shots now start at the visible barrel instead of beyond it.

Close the previous launcher before installing. Open BLACK SOULS II VR from the
Start menu after updating so it can prepare the missing artwork automatically.
