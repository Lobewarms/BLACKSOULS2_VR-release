BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.23

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Steam achievements earned through the original game's events can now count
  while playing on PC or in VR. Use your installed Steam copy and stay signed
  in to Steam. The launcher log shows when achievements are connected.
  Unconfirmed earned requests are kept with your mod save for a later retry.
  Loading older progress does not automatically award every past milestone.
- The clock-tower monster's blinding effect now clears when its fight ends,
  including victories before the monster's scheduled fade.
- The university's central staircase now connects the hall and gallery instead
  of becoming an upright wall. Its upper and side stairs also have connected
  landings, while the original blocked passages and fall routes are preserved.

The river, scenery, atmosphere, dialogue and gameplay fixes from previous
updates are included.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
