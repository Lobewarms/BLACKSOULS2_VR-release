BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.24

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Returning from VR restores the desktop view to the current window without
  stretching. Your position, facing direction and open menus carry across.
- VR entry prepares the monitor view earlier. Leaving a healthy VR session no
  longer restarts the headset connection before you can enter VR again.
- Held controls are cleared when changing modes, so switching does not become
  an attack, movement or menu selection. The VR HUD also works without a left
  controller connected yet.
- Corrected the raised route at Abandoned Station Hanover, the split floor
  in Stomach, the broken metal staircase in the graveyard, and related wooden
  stair and ladder joins in the sewers and Chaos Dungeon.

The Steam achievements, university stairs, clock-tower blinding effect, river,
scenery, atmosphere, dialogue and gameplay fixes from previous updates are included.

Close the game and previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
