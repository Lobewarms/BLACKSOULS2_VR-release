BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.15

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Pick up nearby world items in VR by bringing your hand close and squeezing
  the right grip, or the left grip with an empty hand. Successful grabs vibrate.
- Quick grip squeezes register reliably, including between frames.
- Right-stick interactions aim at the visible item, including items on tables.
- Item messages, choices and collected state follow the original game.
- Main-menu labels remain complete when you move between commands.
- Run like Dodo's Fast setting now increases walking speed and is saved.
- Torture racks lie horizontally with supports, and loose stationary props rest
  on their visible base instead of floating above the floor.

Left grip still guards when you hold a shield. The right-stick-down interaction
remains available for items, people and doors.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
