BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.21

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Boats and water scenery meet the actual river surface instead of hanging
  above it. Invisible water-blocking tiles no longer become solid walls.
- River bridge decks keep connected islands level instead of being mistaken
  for stairs that progressively lower the river and its boats.
- Stationary props, lamps and joined scenery rest on their visible supporting
  feet, with their original artwork and animations preserved.
- Scenery at opposite map edges no longer joins into a misplaced picture.
- Exiting the moon viewer with Escape clears its image correctly, including
  when the browser releases the mouse without sending a key press.

The area atmosphere, dialogue, equipment and gameplay fixes from previous
updates are included.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
