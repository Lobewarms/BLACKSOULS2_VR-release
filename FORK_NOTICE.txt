BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.16

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Weapon instant-death effects and stacking on-hit effects now apply correctly.
- High-hit-count weapon upgrades keep their full attack count.
- Armor evasion, counterattack bonuses and enemy health regeneration now work.
- Damage-reduction piercing effects honor their intended physical/magical type.
- Extra-action bonuses shorten desktop attack and ranged-attack recovery.
- Weapon accuracy penalties and critical evasion now affect real hit rolls.
- Enemy instant-death spells respect your resistances and death-saving effects.

AGI is a combat stat, separate from walking speed. Run like Dodo controls walking.
In VR, melee still requires a physical swing; extra actions do not create hits
while you hold the weapon still.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
