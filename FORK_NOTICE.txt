BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.26

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Repeated event choices open again correctly instead of reusing an earlier
  answer. Victoria's heart meter keeps the original staged progression.
- Pictures from battle events disappear when those events end, without
  deleting unrelated map pictures.
- Creatures without a name in the original game no longer display labels
  such as "Enemy 157". Their health and status effects remain visible.
- Named enemies continue to show their original game names. Changing into
  an unnamed form clears the previous name instead of keeping a stale label.

The natural area colours, VR/desktop switching, terrain, Steam achievements,
clock-tower blinding, scenery, dialogue and gameplay fixes from previous updates
are included.

Close the game and previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
