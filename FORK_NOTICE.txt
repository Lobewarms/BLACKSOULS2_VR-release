BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.20

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Areas have more distinct lighting and visibility: open towns, forests and
  snowy regions no longer share the same dungeon-style fog and carried light.
- Original map colours and grayscale effects appear correctly, with scripted
  lighting changes preserved.
- Rain, snow, drifting leaves, mist and light shafts use the original game's
  weather rules and artwork where available.
- Outdoor areas without a usable sky picture receive suitable original sky
  artwork. The original moon-over-lake panorama also appears correctly.
- Verified forest-border artwork keeps its foliage shapes instead of being
  stretched into opaque building blocks.

The dialogue, equipment and gameplay fixes from previous updates are included.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
