BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.17

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Weapon stun now applies the original Stun status instead of being converted
  into extra stagger buildup. This fixes Great Sword and other stun weapons.
- Successful stuns interrupt enemy attacks and prevent actions until they expire.
- Enemy effect labels show Stun using the original game's icon.
- Stun chances still respect enemy resistance, immunity and luck. Great Sword's
  base chance is 80%; a landed hit is not a guaranteed stun.

Normal stagger and Break mechanics remain available alongside weapon stun.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
