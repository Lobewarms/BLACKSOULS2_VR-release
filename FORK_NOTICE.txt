BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.10

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Enemy names now appear above nearby enemies before you attack them.
- Your HUD and enemy nameplates show status effects, buffs and debuffs.
- Effect names remain visible even when an effect has no icon.
- Extra effects cycle into view; the Status menu lists all your active effects.
- Includes co-op enemy buff indicators and the earlier save-language fixes.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
