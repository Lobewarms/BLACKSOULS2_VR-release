BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.18

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Conversations stay visually stable between dialogue pages and choices.
- The desktop HUD, minimap and held weapons no longer flash back into view
  between messages from the same event.
- The background dimming and mouse control stay consistent through dialogue.
- Normal gameplay presentation returns when the conversation ends or a battle
  starts. Scripted pauses and dialogue choices keep their original behavior.

The weapon stun and gameplay-effect fixes from previous updates are included.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
