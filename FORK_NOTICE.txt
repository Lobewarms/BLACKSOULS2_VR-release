BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.25

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Areas retain the natural colours of the original artwork. Green forest
  floors no longer turn red or orange under a strong screen-wide filter.
- Environmental colour casts and desaturation are gentler throughout the
  world. Nights remain darker with a subtle cool tint; snow stays neutral.
- Weather-driven colour changes and returns from cutscenes use the same
  readable colours. Battle flashes, blinding effects and fades keep their
  original strength and duration.

The VR/desktop switching, terrain, Steam achievements, clock-tower blinding,
river, scenery, dialogue and gameplay fixes from previous updates are included.

Close the game and previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
