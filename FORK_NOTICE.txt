BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.19

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- MP attacks and Drain Mana affect MP correctly; life and mana drains restore
  only the amount available from their target. These changes also sync in co-op.
- Enemy attacks can apply their original weapon effects, including poison,
  bleeding and stun.
- Getting or losing a key or quest item refreshes item-dependent map events.
- Boss-room skulls and other events can open the Save screen correctly.
- Equipment trades require an owned item, equipment restrictions work, and
  events remove the correct number of bag and equipped copies.
- Importing an original-game save preserves remaining buffs, timed effects
  and walking-based status counters.

The dialogue, weapon stun and gameplay fixes from previous updates are included.

Close the previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
