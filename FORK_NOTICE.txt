BLACK SOULS II VR - community version 0.2.3

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- Fixed attacks or guarding staying held after switching windows or releasing the mouse.
- Graphics can load again after a temporary download failure.
- Fixed incorrect decoding of some Japanese text.
- Save-slot errors no longer show occupied slots as empty. Failed saves and loads
  display a message, and a failed load keeps you in the menu.
