BLACK SOULS II VR - How to play

START PLAYING
1. Open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu (or run BlacksoulsVR.exe).
2. Click Browse and choose your original BLACK SOULS II folder, containing Game.exe.
3. Click Launch. Keep the launcher open while playing.
4. On PC, click inside the game window to capture your mouse.

PC CONTROLS
WASD: move             Shift: run             Mouse: look
Left click: attack     Right click: guard     Space: dodge
E / Enter: interact    Tab: menu               Esc: cancel
See CONTROLS_en.txt for all controls.

META QUEST 3 / PC VR
Connect your headset to your Windows PC using Quest Link, Air Link or Virtual
Desktop before launching the game. Click ENTER VR at the bottom of the game window.
Quest needs the PC to run the game; the mod does not run on the headset by itself.

Left stick: move       Right stick: turn      Right-hand swing: attack
Left grip: guard       A: dodge / confirm     Y: menu / B: cancel
Flick the right stick down to interact.

NEED HELP?
You need Windows 10/11, an up-to-date Chrome or Edge, and your original game.
If VR NOT SUPPORTED appears, start Quest Link or SteamVR, close the game window,
then click Launch again. For missing tiles or emotes, the optional RPG Maker
VX Ace RTP may help. README_en.txt contains the full setup guide.

SAVES AND UPDATES
Mod saves are in the saves folder beside BlacksoulsVR.exe. Your original game and
saves are not changed. Setup keeps mod saves during upgrades and uninstall.
Download community updates here:
https://github.com/dimasma0305/BLACKSOULS2_VR-release/releases
The launcher's own updater may install a different version from the original author.
