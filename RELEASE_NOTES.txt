BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.40

Bonus Action chains
- Earn follow-up actions from your Bonus Action traits during combat. Each source
  rolls once after a normal eligible action; follow-ups cannot generate more.
- Remove the three-second activation window. Your follow-ups stay available
  until used or combat ends. The HUD shows the original icon and remaining count.
- Ordinary combat skills share an action window and recovery with attacks, even
  when switching skills or using the R menu. A bonus lets you follow up during
  the previous action's recovery, after its active motion finishes.
- The final follow-up retains its own recovery before a new normal chain starts.
  Costs, skill cooldowns, attack motions and burst spacing still apply.
- Keep instant support skills usable while their menu is open. Field actions
  pause with the menu or headset, and unused bonuses clear when the fight ends.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
