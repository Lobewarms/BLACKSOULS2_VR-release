BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.52

Confusion ending correctly
- Curing or ending Rampage stops queued automatic hits, follow-up skills and
  unlaunched gun rounds, and restores manual attacks and skills immediately.
- Already-fired projectiles and paid weapon-skill cooldowns continue normally.

Combat and event fidelity fixes
- Attacks, skills and projectiles now use the original critical scaling, critical
  evasion, counter, reflection and substitution rules. Each repeated strike gets
  its own chance to hit; weapon arts keep their own authored repeat count.
- Bonus Actions remain available when an ordinary action is already ready.
- Enemy skills respect Silence, seals and resources. An enemy with no usable
  action waits. Rampage forces normal attacks rather than permitting manual arts.
- Delayed strikes, NPC cooldowns, map events, movement routes and timers pause
  with local menus. Companion status durations refresh and survive save/load.
- Nested confirmations preserve the original choice group. Original event
  movies play on PC and in VR; use Play/A/Enter if prompted, or Skip/B/Esc.

Grand Guignol Skills menu
- R opens Skills during Grand Guignol and other battles whose entrances disable
  the exploration menu. Dialogue and cutscenes still control when menus can open.

Bonus Action skill chains
- R Skills shows earned Bonus Actions and your queued skills.
- With extra actions available, select skills, then press R again or click Cast
  to use them in order. In VR, use the Cast button. Remove last and Clear edit
  the plan; Esc/Back cancels it without spending actions or resources.
- Each cast spends one earned action and uses its own costs and cooldowns.
  An unavailable skill stops the remaining chain without spending for that cast.
  Temporary weapon switching preserves earned actions and uses the art's stats.

Lindamea battle
- Cinematic backsteps no longer slow your movement during real-time combat.
- Her shared immortality phase shows its remaining time below the enemy HP bar.
  The countdown pauses with the fight when a blocking menu is open.

Performance improvements
- More responsive Skills browsing with large skill lists and many owned weapon arts.
  Costs, cooldowns and skill requirements stay up to date while you browse.
- Reduced repeated work in maps with many exits or scenery events, while keeping
  changes to events, routes and map artwork responsive.
- Reduced rendering work for scenery outside the camera view in large areas,
  preserving the original artwork, appearance and collision.

Skill cooldowns during menus
- Skill cooldowns and shared action recovery pause with the world, including
  while R Skills is open. Return to gameplay to resume them.
- Accepted action-consuming skills, including healing and buffs, return to the
  game automatically. Reopening R freezes their remaining recovery too.
  Pure out-of-combat utility can stay open. Native durations and costs still apply.

Use arts from owned weapons
- R Skills includes arts from weapons, offhand guns and shields in your bag. See the source
  weapon's original icon/name and the costs for the selected action.
- Use a compatible source weapon for that action, then resume your usual gear.
  Repeats and projectiles retain its weapon stats and effects; normal equipment,
  cost, target, recovery and cooldown requirements still apply.
- Borrowed-only arts are used directly from R. Equip their source weapon before
  assigning to a quick slot; learned/currently granted skills keep normal Q access.
- Main profile stats show current equipment; the selected action lists its weapon
  and costs separately. Search also finds source weapon names.
- Compatible equipped gear is preferred, with the highest owned +N when swapping
  within a weapon family. Break still prepares the next real hit for three seconds;
  disarming cancels its preparation.

Finding and using skills
- Browse Favorites, All, Weapon Arts, Attack, Healing, Buffs and Debuffs.
  Favorites appear first; each character remembers their view and selection.
- Press / or click Search to find skills on PC. Click a skill or press Enter
  to use it directly; while typing in search, Enter/Esc returns to the list.
- Press F to favorite, or Q to assign a quick slot. VR has visible controls
  for categories, favorites and assignment alongside its normal navigation.
- Unavailable skills remain inspectable with current costs and reasons.
  The party leader profile remains visible during browsing and assignment.
- Read two lines of the original skill description, with longer text available
  on the profile pages. Compact windows use category arrows and full labels.

Your profile in Skills
- Open Skills with R on PC to see your party leader's original portrait,
  HP/MP/TP, stamina and all eight current stats alongside skill selection.
- Page through every active status effect and buff with original game icons,
  useful modifiers and healing warnings. Selected skill costs, cooldowns and
  reasons it cannot be used remain visible while browsing or assigning skills.
- Use Previous/Next, Page Up/Page Down or the mouse wheel over the profile.
  VR uses the page buttons; smaller PC windows stack and page the profile.

Old King shadow phase
- Fix successful Breaks not counting when the stagger ends before the boss's
  original turn-end check, including when a follow-up attack ends the stagger.
- Keep the original Break chance, stagger duration and six-shadow requirement.
  Each shadow counts once; ordinary damage and stun do not replace Break.

Chaos Dungeon skull saves
- Includes the fix allowing skull checkpoints to open their save screen while
  ordinary menu saving is disabled, with slot selection and overwrite checks.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
