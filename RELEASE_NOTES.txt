BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.36

VR doors and conversations
- Keep nearby doors and people usable when right-controller tracking is lost.
- Use your view direction when the controller has no usable horizontal aim.
- Talk across original counters without having to turn your head in the same direction.
- Keep touch doors aligned with controller aim and preserve the original door locks.

In VR, point toward a nearby door or person and flick the right thumbstick down
to Examine/Talk. If the controller is not tracked, look toward the target instead.
The trigger remains the weapon control.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
