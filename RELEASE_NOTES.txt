BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.37

Buy larger quantities
- Buy more than 99 items in one purchase, up to your available capacity and funds.
- Your bag holds up to 9,999 of each item; storage keeps its own item limits.
- Use +100 / -100 and +1,000 / -1,000 for larger batches, or choose Max.
- Max respects Souls and every required exchange material. Selecting Max does not
  buy immediately: confirm the quantity on the first row to complete the purchase.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
