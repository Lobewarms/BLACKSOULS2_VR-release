BLACK SOULS II VR - 0.2.27

GET STARTED
Run the Windows setup EXE, then open BLACK SOULS II VR from the Start menu.
The app looks for your game automatically. Confirm the folder and click Launch.
If it cannot find your game, click Browse to choose it.
For VR, connect your headset to the PC first, then click ENTER VR in the game window.

WHAT'S NEW
- NPC nameplates now use the translations in your installed game, including
  Ghost Receptionist and other names previously left in Japanese.
- Translated names remain visible when switching PC/VR layouts or resizing.
- Area labels, original-save lists and co-op locations use game display names
  instead of internal map editor labels.
- The launcher, setup and player guides use the BLACK SOULS II VR project name.

The natural area colours, VR/desktop switching, terrain, Steam achievements,
clock-tower blinding, scenery, dialogue, event choices, picture cleanup and
gameplay fixes from previous updates are included.

Close the game and previous launcher before installing, then reopen BLACK SOULS II VR
from the Start menu.
