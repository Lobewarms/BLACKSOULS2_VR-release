BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.53

- Improved Quest Link startup compatibility with current Chrome and Edge, with
  longer connection waits and the existing cancellation and recovery controls.
- More natural chained sword cuts and charged thrusts on PC; VR blades respond
  to weapon weight, with an adjustable weight setting.
- Updated stamina costs, dodge and attack buffering, shield presentation and
  multiplayer stagger rules. R Skills, Bonus Actions and paused cooldowns remain.
- Updated Lindamea attacks and restored lift platforms and university scenery.
- Uses the original game's fonts, window colors and scrolling-text behavior.
  Added Russian launcher and in-game menus, plus two-line item descriptions.
- Multiplayer text and voice chat, microphone controls, mute controls and shared
  loot. Voice transmission requires enabling your microphone in the chat menu.
- Preserves the existing save handling, game discovery, updater, portrait/HUD,
  terrain, status-effect and combat fixes. Original game files are not included.

Download the setup EXE for Windows, or the portable ZIP. Install over your
existing mod installation to keep its saves and settings. Keep the launcher
open while playing. Headset startup depends on Quest Link/SteamVR being ready.
