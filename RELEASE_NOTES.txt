BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.39

Bonus Action charges
- Bonus Action traits now roll for extra actions instead of passively speeding
  up attacks. Each source rolls separately: +50% is a 50% chance for one charge;
  +100% guarantees one. Multiple sources can award multiple charges.
- Use the next attack or eligible combat skill within three seconds of active
  play to skip normal action recovery. The timer pauses in menus and dialogue.
- A small HUD indicator shows the original game icon, charges and time remaining.
- Works with PC attacks, VR swings and skills used directly through the R menu.
- Resource costs, skill cooldowns, active attack motion and burst spacing still
  apply. Bonus actions cannot generate more charges; utility actions do not count.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
