BLACK SOULS II VR 0.2.38

Skills shortcut and immediate use
- Press R on PC to open Skills directly. Press R again to close it.
- E remains Examine/Talk.
- Face a nearby visible enemy, open Skills and choose Use to cast or strike
  immediately. No valid target means no cost. Quick slots retain manual aiming.
- Healing, support and utility skills keep their normal requirements. Dodge and
  manual Break retain their dedicated actions.

Dialogue cleanup
- Clear an acknowledged dialogue page when a title banner replaces it, including
  the intro poem before arriving in the Crash Chamber.
- Click a plain dialogue box, or point and pull the VR trigger, to advance it.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
