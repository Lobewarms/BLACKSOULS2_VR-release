Entering VR
- Request the headset connection immediately when you click ENTER VR.
- Avoid repeating a completed graphics switch while opening the headset view.
- Show connection progress and let you cancel while VR is starting.
- Report when the headset is connected but still waiting for tracking.
- Retry a slow VR exit without opening a competing headset session.
- Keep an unprepared desktop spectator view from uploading the map during VR play.

If a connection remains stalled, follow the message beside ENTER VR.
Check that Quest Link or SteamVR is connected, then try again.
Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
