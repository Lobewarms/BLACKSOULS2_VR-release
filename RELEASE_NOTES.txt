Slow connections and stuck VR exits
- Give a slow headset connection time to finish instead of discarding it after 20 seconds.
- Keep one connection in progress while cancellation or shutdown is unfinished.
- Offer Reset VR connection after a stalled startup or exit.
- Wait for actual closure before allowing another entry; do not reload the game automatically.
- Keep clearer progress and recovery instructions in all four supported languages.

If Quest Link itself freezes, restart the headset and Link, and check the
Meta Link PC app for graphics-driver warnings. A game update cannot repair a
frozen headset service or change its driver.

Install the update, then close and reopen the game window to load the changes.
Your existing saves are retained. The original game is required and is not included.
