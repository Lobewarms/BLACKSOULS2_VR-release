BLACK SOULS II VR - Controls  (version 2026.10.22-17)
================================================

VR
--
Move              Left stick (click the stick: run / walk)
Turn              Right stick left / right
Attack            Swing the weapon in your right hand (guns, bows, the staff that shoots magic: right trigger)
Charged blow      Hold the right trigger, then swing
Break             Hold the left trigger and swing with your right hand (a gun in the shield slot: left trigger fires)
Guard             Left grip - shield toward the attack (raise it just before the hit: perfect guard)
Dodge             A
Disarm            Click the right stick
Examine / talk    Flick the right stick down
Jump              Flick the right stick up
Quick slots       Hold X, move your left hand onto a slot, release X
Spells / throws   Pick from the quick slots, aim with your right hand, right trigger
Weapon arts       Pick from the quick slots, then hit an enemy with the weapon
Emotes            Hold B, move your right hand onto a balloon, release B
Chat (co-op)      Release B on the emote ring's speech-balloon slot: a keyboard (or Menu -> Co-op -> Chat and voice)
Resins            Held in the left hand: rub along the blade of the right-hand weapon
Herb bottle       Held in the hand: bring it to your mouth
Blood vial        Held in the hand: stab it into your body
Menu              Y
Confirm           A (or point with the laser, right trigger)
Cancel            B
In menus          Right stick (number input: left / right = digit, up / down = value)
Weapon hold       Menu -> weapon / shield model adjust

PC
--
Move              W A S D (Shift: run)
Look              Mouse (click the game view to lock it)
Attack            Left click (hold and release: charged blow)
Break             Hold F (or the wheel button) and left click
Guard             Right click
Dodge             Space
Disarm            H
Jump              C
Examine / talk /  E / Enter
confirm
Quick slots       Hold Q and pick with the mouse / keys 1-8
Emotes            Hold T and pick with the mouse
Chat (co-op)      / (Enter sends, Esc closes)
Menu              Tab
Cancel            Esc
In menus          Arrow keys (number input: left / right = digit, up / down = value)
